Live observations

Add apparatus to begin. There is no Run button: observations update as you build and connect the setup.

What makes this interactive?

Physics and optics setups respond to selected apparatus; chemistry displays simulated indicator / mixture observations; biology displays cell and process observations. Electrical components use visible wires and a simple continuity model. These are educational models, not precision lab instruments.
